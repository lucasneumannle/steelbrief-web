Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This policy explains what information SteelBrief ("we", "us") collects when you visit steelbrief.co, request a sample, start a trial, subscribe, or correspond with us — and how we use it. We keep this simple because our data practices are simple: we collect the minimum needed to deliver a weekly brief.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide: name, email address, and company (when you request a sample, start a trial, subscribe, or email us).
  • Subscription and billing data: processed by our merchant of record, Freemius, Inc. We receive subscription status, plan, and billing country. We never receive or store your card number — payment details go directly to Freemius and its payment processors (see the Freemius privacy notice at checkout).
  • Delivery data: the workbook is delivered as a Google Sheet shared with your email address via Google Workspace; standard Google access logs apply on Google's side.
  • Email interaction data: standard delivery/open metadata from our email tooling, used to operate and improve deliverability.
  • Basic web analytics: aggregate, non-identifying traffic data via our hosting/CDN provider (Cloudflare). We do not run advertising trackers.

2. How we use it

  • To deliver the Service: sending the weekly brief, sharing the subscriber workbook, managing trials and subscriptions;
  • To respond to your requests and provide support;
  • To send relevant business correspondence, including outreach to professional addresses; every marketing email includes a working unsubscribe option and our sender information, and opt-outs are honored promptly;
  • To protect the Service (fraud and abuse prevention) and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties except the service providers below.

3. Service providers

We use a small set of processors to operate the Service: Freemius (payments and subscription management), Zoho (email), Google Workspace (workbook delivery via Google Sheets), Cloudflare (hosting/CDN), GitHub (site deployment), and our email delivery tooling. Each receives only the data needed for its function.

4. Data retention

We keep subscriber contact and subscription records while your subscription or trial is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for accounting and legal purposes. If you unsubscribe from emails, we retain your address on a suppression list so we don't contact you again.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time via the link in any message or by emailing us;
  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information — email hello@steelbrief.co and we will act on verified requests within 30 days;
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU/EEA), you may have additional statutory rights, including the right to know what personal information we hold and to request its deletion. We honor such requests regardless of location.

6. International transfers

We operate internationally; information may be processed in the United States, Brazil, and other countries where our providers operate, under those providers' standard safeguards.

7. Security

We use reputable providers with industry-standard security (TLS in transit, access controls, two-factor authentication on our systems). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we minimize what we collect in the first place.

8. Children

The Service is a B2B product intended for business professionals and is not directed to anyone under 18.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; changes appear on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Questions or requests: hello@steelbrief.co.
